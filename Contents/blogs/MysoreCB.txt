The Infrastructure Catalyst: The Expressway Effect
The key factor that will impact the future of real estate in Mysore is the construction of the 10-lane expressway connecting Mysuru and Bengaluru. Reducing commute time from almost four hours to less than 90 minutes, the expressway has made Mysore a logical economic extension of Bengaluru city.

Here’s how:

Commuter & Hybrid Working Change: IT professionals employed in South-Western areas of Bengaluru can now live in Mysore or maintain a secondary home without facing any commute issues.

Airport Development: The development at Mandakalli Airport has improved air connectivity in the region, making the city more approachable to business travelers as well as NRIs.

Civic Development: The expansion of the civic infrastructure planned under the Greater Mysuru masterplan is improving infrastructure facilities like water supply and the ring road network.

The Rise of IT and Industrial Ecosystems
Mysore is no longer relying solely on tourism. The city is rapidly emerging as Karnataka’s designated second technology hub.

Anchor campuses like the Infosys Development Centre, alongside major industrial parks in Hebbal, Hootagalli, and Kadakola, are attracting tech firms, manufacturing giants, and semiconductor setups. 

As corporate expansion creates thousands of white-collar jobs, the demand for high-quality properties in Mysore is experiencing unprecedented momentum.

Apartment Culture Is Gaining Ground
Historically dominated by independent sites and plotted developments, Mysore’s housing preferences are undergoing a major shift. Professionals, migrating workforce, and even retired people are now increasingly drawn towards living in gated communities.

The demand for apartments in Mysore has risen because of several factors:

Low Maintenance Lifestyle: The gated towers come with security, backup electricity, and professional management of facilities.
Luxury Amenities: Modern developments include clubhouses, pools, gyms, and green areas that meet modern-day requirements.
Profitable Investment: As corporate professionals relocate for work, buyers looking for an apartment for sale in Mysore can secure steady rental returns alongside long-term capital appreciation. 
Top Micro-Markets to Watch in Mysore
Not all neighborhoods perform equally. Strategic real estate allocation depends on identifying corridors with high infrastructure visibility:

Hunsur Road & Ring Road Junction: The most highly favored growth belt owing to its closeness to key IT campuses, educational institutions, and healthcare establishments.
Hebbal Industrial Corridor: Very highly favored by corporate professionals and investors who want stable rental income near industrial and technology parks.
Vijayanagar & Dattagalli: Already established residential localities that are known for their premium social infrastructure and large roadways.
Bogadi Road & Srirampura: New emerging locations providing great growth opportunity for early bird buyers and plotted projects.
Buyer’s Due Diligence Checklist
If you are planning to buy property either for personal use or investment purposes in an upcoming tier-2 city, here are some points to consider.
