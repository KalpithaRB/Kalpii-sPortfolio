I’m a writer who notices the quiet details most people walk right past; the pauses in a conversation, the small habits that give someone away, and the unspoken feelings sitting between the lines. Whether I’m crafting brand copy or building fictional worlds, I write to make readers pause and actually feel something.

My field experience started with four months of freelancing, where I scripted reels and designed carousels for a fitness coach, learning how to hook an audience in seconds. Right now, I’m wrapping up a four-month content writing internship at a digital marketing agency, writing everything from Meta ad copies and website pages to blogs, LinkedIn newsletters, and press releases. What sets my writing apart is that I don't write for algorithms; I write like a real person talking to another person, choosing genuine emotion and sharp observation over recycled marketing buzzwords.

When I’m off the clock, my current obsession is tearing through crime thriller novels, though I still reach for a slice-of-life story every now and then, like a warm sunny day breaking through a harsh winter. Outside of reading and writing, I’m always up for an art-based challenge or chasing down a brand-new hobby. I genuinely love creating things and learning from scratch, even if I’m nowhere near perfect at them yet.
